Starterling

A friendly companion for your sourdough starter. It reminds you when your starter peaks and when it's hungry, works out your feedings in grams, keeps a history and walks beginners through making a starter in 7 days. Pay once, no subscription. Works offline.

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to starterling.app@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what you were doing.

Common questions

How do I restore Starterling Pro on a new phone?

Open the Pro offer from the Starter tab and tap “Restore purchase”, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Why did a reminder arrive a few minutes late?

Android may batch scheduled notifications to save battery, which can delay them by a few minutes. Make sure notifications are on for Starterling in your phone settings.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives on your phone. Press and hold a starter in the grid to delete it, or uninstall the app to remove everything. See the privacy policy.

Perguntas frequentes

Como recupero o Starterling Pro num celular novo?

Abra a oferta do Pro na aba Fermento e toque em “Restaurar compra”, usando o mesmo Apple ID ou a mesma conta Google da compra.

Por que um lembrete chegou alguns minutos atrasado?

O Android pode juntar notificações agendadas para economizar bateria, o que atrasa alguns minutos. Confira se as notificações do Starterling estão ligadas nas configurações do celular.

Como apago meus dados?

Tudo fica no seu celular. Segure um fermento na grade para apagá-lo, ou desinstale o app para remover tudo. Veja a política de privacidade.
